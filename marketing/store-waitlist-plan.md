# Store waitlist — marketing plan (demand capture)

**Goal:** drive qualified traffic to the per-model waitlist forms so you get (1)
real **demand-by-model** data and (2) a warm launch list — *before* building
anything. Success isn't traffic; it's **signups per model, and which content drove
them.** The winner gets built first.

> This plan is for the **retail store models** (DCF, Monte Carlo, Dividend,
> Options, Business-Plan kit). The **RIA/advisor Monte Carlo venture** (Wall of
> Indifference) is a *different* motion — direct outreach via the 300-firm call kit,
> not this content funnel. Don't conflate the two.

---

## The core mechanic: content matched to model

Don't advertise "a store." Publish the piece that makes someone *feel* the need for
a model, then offer the model as the payoff. You're already writing these issues —
the waitlist CTA just rides them. The content that converts tells you the demand.

## Channels (organic-first, per the growth-engine firewall)

1. **Newsletter — primary.** Every issue that matches a model ends with a soft
   waitlist CTA to *that* model. Free content proves quality; the waitlist rides it.
2. **LinkedIn — your audience.** Repurpose the issue's *Signal vs. Noise* teardown
   as a native post; put the store/waitlist link in the **first comment** (LinkedIn
   throttles outbound links in the body).
3. **X/Twitter thread** — same teardown, link at the end.
4. **Site cross-links** — `index.html` and `insights.html` point to the store; the
   store points relevant readers to the matching free issue (two-way funnel).

---

## Cadence (4-week rolling, tied to the editorial calendar)

Rotate models so each gets its own demand test:

- **Each week:** publish the matched issue → same-day LinkedIn post + X thread →
  waitlist CTA for that week's model.
- **Week 1:** DCF · **Week 2:** Dividend · **Week 3:** Options · **Week 4:** Monte
  Carlo. (Business-Plan kit rides founder-flavored posts opportunistically.)
- Repeat the rotation; each pass is another read on demand.

---

## Measurement & the decision rule (this is the whole point)

- **Track signups per model weekly** (each Formspree form = one model's tally).
- **Build order = demand order.** The model that crosses the bar first gets built
  first. Everything else waits.
- **Suggested trigger to build:** first model to **~50–100 waitlist emails**, or a
  cluster of "I'd pay for this" replies. Tune to your gut once real numbers land.
- **Kill signal:** a model whose matched content ran and produced ~nothing is telling
  you not to build it. That's a *win* — you just saved the build.

---

## Offer mechanics (honest scarcity — the firewall holds)

- Waitlist reward is **real:** founding price + first access, **capped** founding
  cohort. Genuine scarcity, not a fake countdown.
- **No hype, no return/income promises, no fabricated signup counts.** Prices shown
  are the honest planned launch prices. The integrity *is* the differentiator.

---

## Paid amplification (optional, only after organic converts)

Once *one* model converts organically: add a conversion pixel on the store, set a
**small daily test budget you're comfortable losing**, run credibility-led ads (real
CRD, zero promises) that push the matched free content, and optimize on
**cost-per-waitlist-signup.** Let the content do the selling; ads just widen the top.
