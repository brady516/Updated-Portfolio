Privacy Policy

What we collect, why, and your control over it. Last updated: July 2026. Questions? Reach out.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join a product waitlist, subscribe to the newsletter, or contact us.
  • Anything you choose to tell us in a form or email (e.g., what you'd want a model to do).
  • Payment information only at purchase — handled entirely by our payment processor (Stripe). We never see or store your full card details.
  • Basic, standard web/analytics data your browser sends (e.g., approximate location, device/browser type) if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To email you about the specific product or list you signed up for.
  • To deliver and support products you buy.
  • To decide, from aggregate waitlist demand, which products to build first.

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Who processes your data (subprocessors)

We use reputable third parties to run the Service; your data may pass through them:

  • Formspree — waitlist / contact form submissions.
  • Stripe — payment processing (at launch).
  • Substack — newsletter hosting and email delivery.
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security.

Each has its own privacy practices governing the data it processes.

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe anytime via the link in any email.
  • Access, correct, or delete your data — email us and we'll honor it, subject to any legal record-keeping requirements.

Data retention & security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we take reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: contact us from the email associated with your data.
