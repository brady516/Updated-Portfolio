Go Legit Local — logo concepts

Quick SVG concept directions to react to — not final art. Colors: navy #0f1b2d · safety-orange #ff6b1a · cream · gold.

1 · Union "Local No." badge recommended

Why this one. "Local" reads as a union local — heritage, blue-collar, trustworthy. The No. 34 device is the wink. Works as a stamp on the site, invoices, email signature, truck decal. The number is yours to pick (34 is a placeholder). A wrench/star mark could sit center if you want it busier.

2 · Vintage script + wrap-under tail (Dodgers-style)

The honest caveat. A true Dodgers-style script needs a real script typeface converted to outlines — this is an italic-serif approximation with the wrap-under swoosh you described. Good enough to judge the direction; a designer (or a licensed script font) makes it sing.

3 · Compact mark (favicon / app / avatar)

The badge distilled to a square for a browser tab, phone icon, or social avatar. Pairs with either wordmark above.
