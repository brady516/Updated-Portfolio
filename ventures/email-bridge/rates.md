# Rates — Go Legit Local

## Pricing method (read this before setting a number)

**Price on VALUE, verify on HOURS. Never cost-plus.**

Counting your hours and multiplying by a rate is the freelancer trap. You built an
AI-templated process so the hours *shrink* — if you price on hours, every efficiency
gain forces you to charge *less*. That's punishing yourself for being good. The customer
buys the **outcome** (look legit, get found, stop losing bids), not your time.

Use hours for exactly two things:
1. **The cost floor** — never price below what your time is worth.
2. **The effective-hourly sanity check** — is this worth doing vs. the $299 email
   (which runs ~$900/hr)? And what margin survives when a VA delivers it? (That's the
   exit math.)

## The hours, counted (so you can see the margin)

Full **$2,997 package**, once your process is dialed:

| Task | ~Hours |
|---|---|
| AI-generated multi-page site + content + 1 revision | 3.0 |
| Email setup (`provision.py` + activation call) | 0.5 |
| Google Business Profile setup/optimize | 1.0 |
| Lead-capture form | 0.5 |
| AI-fit session | 0.5 |
| SEO-foundations walkthrough | 0.75 |
| Onboarding/handoff + cheat sheet | 0.75 |
| **Total** | **~7 hrs** |

- **Effective rate: ~$2,997 ÷ 7 ≈ $428/hr.** Healthy — and it *rises* as your process
  gets faster, because you priced on value, not hours.
- **VA math (the exit):** at a $30/hr VA, delivery costs ~$210 → you keep **~93%
  margin** even when you're not the one doing it. *That's* what makes it a sellable
  asset, not a job.

Contrast: a local agency charges **$5,000–$10,000** for a site plus ongoing fees. You're
the honest fraction of that, you deliver in days, and they *own it.* That's the anchor —
not your hours.

## How to present price (don't undercut yourself)

- **Anchor first.** "Agencies run five to ten grand and hold your site hostage." Then
  $2,997 lands as the deal it is. (See `conversion-psychology.md` — real anchors only.)
- **Quote the package, never the hours.** The moment you say "it's about 7 hours," they
  do the math and haggle your rate. You're selling the result, not the clock.
- **One number, said plainly, then stop talking.** Confidence in the price *is* part of
  the price.
- **Deposit 50% on Tier 2/3** to start (see `rules-of-engagement.md`); Tier 1 pays in
  full.

tell you the floor and the margin.*
