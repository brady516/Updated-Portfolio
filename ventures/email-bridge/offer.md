# Cash bridge — "Look Legit" professional email setup

**What this is:** a stupidly simple, fast-yes, flat-fee service that gets a trades /
small-business owner off `business@gmail.com` and onto professional domain email
(`you@yourbusiness.com`), done-for-them. **Purpose: cash *now* to fund the slow RIA
sales cycle. It is a bridge, not a company. It has a sunset.**

**Why it stays open (the moat):** it's too unsexy for competent operators to touch,
so the only competition is the Fiverr price-race with no positioning or trust. You
win on **niche + looking legit + being the specialist**, not on skill (the skill is
commodity). Deviously niched.

> **Keep it off North de Noise.** Do NOT put "email for welders" on your
> institutional-finance domain — it dilutes the RIA brand. Run it under a separate,
> cheap, single-purpose landing page / brand. Different audience, different face.

## Delivery checklist (the repeatable ~45-minute machine)

Batch these into one time block. Same steps every time:

1. Collect from client: domain (or buy one — add-on), the inbox they want mail in,
   name/title/phone/logo for signature.
2. Point DNS / enable free routing (Cloudflare Email Routing or equivalent) → forward
   to their inbox.
3. Set up "Send mail as" (app password + SMTP) so they send from the pro address.
4. Add **SPF / DKIM / DMARC** records.
5. Build + install the signature.
6. Test the full loop (send + receive + not-spam), send the cheat sheet, invoice paid.
7. Close ticket. Done. No lingering.

*(You already ran this exact process on your own domain — that's the whole product.)*

## Distribution (where fast $300 yeses come from)

The offer is a slam dunk; the only real work is putting it in front of people who
decide fast. Lowest-effort channels first:

1. **Referral partners who see the Gmail on invoices:** bookkeepers, accountants,
   local web designers. Offer them a **$50 kickback per referral.** They have the
   customers; you have the fix. This is the channel — everything else is retail.
2. **Trade Facebook groups / subreddits** (r/electricians, r/HVAC, local contractor
   groups): "I get tradespeople off gmail@ and onto pro email in 48h, flat $299."
3. **Referrals + whole-crew upsell:** every happy owner knows 5 others and has techs.
4. **Chamber / BNI / local business groups** if you're already in any.

Pick **one trade to start** (whichever you have any connection to) and own it — "the
email guy for [trade]" beats "email guy for everyone." Niche = faster yes + word of
mouth.

## The sunset rule (non-negotiable)

A bridge has two ends. **The day RIA (or newsletter) recurring revenue covers your
monthly nut, you kill this or hand it to a VA with the checklist.** If you don't set
that trigger now, a $299 side-gig quietly becomes your job and eats the hours meant
for the real, defensible bet. Write the number down: *when MRR ≥ $____, sunset.*
