# FAQ — Go Legit Local

**Voice:** warm, plain, humble — a neighbor, not an agency. (Deliberately different
from North de Noise's sharper voice; this audience wants a straight-talking human.)

An FAQ's real job is to kill the specific fears that stop a purchase. Ordered so the
biggest ones (cost, ownership, "am I capable") come first.

---

**What do you actually do?**
We handle the backend tech stuff you're too busy for — a simple website you own and can
edit, a professional email (`you@yourbusiness.com`, set up in the inbox you already
use), getting you found on Google, and the rules of thumb to keep it all running. We're
the easy button for the online side of your business, so you can stay focused on the
actual work.

**How much does it cost?**
Straight answer: professional email setup is a **flat $299, one time.** Websites and the
full digital-presence package are more, and we'll tell you the **exact number on the
call, before you commit a dime.** No mystery invoices, ever.

**Do I own my website and email?**
**Yes — 100%, in your name.** Your domain, your accounts, your logins. We don't hold
anything hostage and there's no lock-in. When we're done, you don't even *need* us.
That's not how most outfits do it — it's how *we* do it.

**Do I have to be good with computers?**
Nope — that's the whole point. If you were, you wouldn't need us. You fill out one form,
hop on one call, and we do the technical part. If you can send a text, you're fine.

**Who's your typical customer?**
Small business owners — usually sole proprietors — who are great at their trade and just
don't have time for the tech. Picture a 60-year-old plumber who's the best in town at
the job but never had a spare minute to sort out his email. That's exactly who we help.

**How does it work?**
We start every project with a phone call — usually about 30 minutes. We listen to your
story and figure out if we can actually help. If we can't, we'll say so. If we can, we
get to work. Call us today.

**How long does it take?**
Email's done in about **48 hours.** A website takes a bit longer, and we'll give you a
real timeline on the call — not "someday."

**Do you use AI to build the websites?**
We sure do — it's how we keep it fast and affordable. **And we show you how to control
it yourself,** so it stays yours to run. Sound good?

**What does your SEO package entail?**
It's a simple **monthly plan.** We do the handful of things that actually move your
Google ranking — your **Google Business Profile**, your reviews, the basics on your
site. And here's the part nobody else does: **we show you exactly what we're doing and
why,** so no "SEO expert" can ever sell you smoke. Contact us to learn more.

**Will you lock me into a long contract?**
No. Setups are one-time. The monthly plans (SEO, support) you can **cancel anytime** —
they earn your business month to month, or they don't deserve it.

**What if something breaks later?**
For **30 days** after setup, if something we built isn't working, we fix it free. After
that, we're glad to help for a small flat fee (we'll always tell you the cost first), or
you can join a low monthly care plan if you'd rather not think about it.

**Do you offer 24-hour support?**
Sorry, but no — we're humans, and we need sleep too. We help you manage the backend so
your business runs smoother, but we're not a 24/7 hotline. We'll always get back to you
quick, though.

**Why should I trust you?**
Fair question. We're straight with you: clear prices, everything in your name, no
lock-in, and we tell you how everything works so you're never in the dark. We'd rather
lose a sale being honest than win one being slick. Ask us anything on the call.
