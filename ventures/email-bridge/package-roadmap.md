# Offer roadmap — the digital-presence package ladder

Brady's draft, structured. The move: **don't sell one mega-package — sell a ladder.**
Email is the wedge (fast cash, automatable, the existing machine); the website/AI
package is the premium ascension. Meet the customer where they are.

Model for the premium sales page: the White Wolf Tours structure — premium, story-led,
benefit-first (not a feature list).

---

## The ladder

| Tier | Includes | Price | Delivery | Automatable? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 — Professional Email** | you@yourbiz.com, done-for-you | **$299** | ~20 min (provision.py) | ✅ fully |
| **2 — Presence Starter** | Tier 1 + one-page AI-built site + Google Business Profile | **~$1,299** | ~2–3 hrs | ⚠️ mostly (templated) |

**Email leads. The package ascends.** Fast yes funds the slow, higher-ticket sale — and
the Care plan MRR is what lifts the exit multiple.

## Scope guardrails (keep it lean + honest)

- **Websites = AI-generated from a fixed template.** One-page (Tier 2) / few-page
  (Tier 3), **one round of edits**, then it's theirs to run. That's the "AI" angle *and*
  the productization that keeps delivery fast. No custom design, no "what shade of blue."
- **Don't sell SEO *results* — sell SEO *honesty.*** Deliver the few things that truly
  move local ranking (Google Business Profile, reviews, basic on-page) and *teach them
  what's real*, so no marketer can con them. Never promise rankings — that's the one
  thing that breaks the integrity bar (and it's unenforceable anyway).
- **"AI guidance" = an honest 30-min session**, not a product. What actually helps their
  business vs. what's hype. Cheap to deliver (your expertise), high perceived value,
  and it's the anti-snake-oil positioning again.

## PRIVATE SUPPORT — what they get (the filled 1–8)

1. **A professional website you own and can edit yourself** — no monthly hostage, no
   agency holding your login.
2. **Your own professional email** (you@yourbusiness.com), set up and working in the
   inbox you already use.
3. **Google Business Profile set up & optimized** — the single biggest thing that
   actually gets a local trade *found.*
4. **Lead capture in one place** — a form so inquiries stop dying in texts and
   voicemails; the start of a real sales process.
5. **A plain-English walkthrough of what actually moves local search** — so no "SEO
   expert" can ever take you for a ride.
6. **A one-on-one "where AI fits your business" session** — honest read on what helps
   you and what's hype. No jargon.
7. **Everything in YOUR name** — domain, accounts, logins. You own all of it, no
   lock-in, no getting held hostage.
8. **A simple cheat sheet + break-fix support** if you get stuck — help when you need
   it, never a forced monthly retainer.

## Premium sales page structure (White Wolf model)

For Tier 3, story-led beats feature-list. Sections, in order:
1. **Hook** — the pain: looking amateur online costs you the jobs your work deserves.
2. **Empathy** — "you mastered a trade, not technology; that's not a failing."
3. **The transformation** — what "handled and in control" looks and feels like.
4. **What you get** — the PRIVATE SUPPORT 1–8, as outcomes.
5. **The anti-rip-off promise** — you own everything; we teach you; no lock-in.
6. **Proof** — first case studies (earned in the pilot).
7. **The offer + guarantee** — clear price, deliverable guarantee.
8. **One CTA, repeated** — "Call us to learn more."

## How it plugs into the machine

- **Same funnel, same SOP.** Email is Stage-3's default offer for *has-site/bad-email*
  leads; the package is for *no-site* leads and warm upsells (SOP already segments this).
- **Same rails** — leadfinder, phone-close (extend objections for the package),
  provision.py (email portion), landing page (add package tiers).
- **Delivery for the site portion:** an AI site builder + fixed template + the
  provisioning script for email/DNS. Keep the checklist discipline; keep it remote.
- **Sunset/exit unchanged** — the Care-plan MRR from this ladder is the multiple-lifter.
